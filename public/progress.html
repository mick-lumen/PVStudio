Synthetic WebODM house

A deterministic CC0 stand-in with a pitched roof, four walls, surrounding ground, MTL materials, and three JPEG maps. On the public fallback, the sample request returned the OBJ, MTL, and all three JPEG maps with HTTP 200 and rendered metadata of 1,097,280 vertices, 365,760 polygons, and 7 meshes.
